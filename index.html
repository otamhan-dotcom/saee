About

Precision, preservation and evidence-based practice.

I am Dr. Saee Anawalikar, a resident in Conservative Dentistry and Endodontics with a clinical focus on microendodontics, restorative dentistry, and aesthetic dental care.

My journey in dentistry began in Pune, where I completed my undergraduate training as a college topper, before moving to Chennai to pursue my passion for Endodontics.

My approach to dentistry is centred on precision, preservation, and evidence-based practice, with an emphasis on conserving natural tooth structure and achieving predictable, long-term outcomes.

I believe dentistry is a continuous process of learning and refinement. As I move forward, I hope to combine evidence-based endodontics, clinical excellence, innovation, and patient-centred care while continuing to grow both as a clinician and as an individual.
